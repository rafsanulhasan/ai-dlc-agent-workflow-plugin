## 0. Design decisions you need first

**D-A. AC parts (MAJOR; this is refine R-2).** The brief's rule, "every AC delivered by a bolt belongs to exactly one story", can't hold literally. §16.1 maps 26 ACs to more than one bolt (for example AC-15.2 to 002, 007 and 008), and a story belongs to one bolt. The units' own *Split rule* (split by file ownership) already implies the answer:
- a multi-bolt AC is split into **parts**, written `AC-15.2[hook]`;
- each part belongs to exactly one story;
- an AC is met when all its parts are met.

Counts:
- 85 ACs are bolt-delivered;
- 59 of them go whole to one story;
- 26 are split into 69 parts;
- that makes 128 assignments, each in exactly one story;
- the other 19 ACs are not story ACs: 16 are spec-only or intent-level, and 3 are the bolt Definition of Done (D-B). 85 + 19 = 104.

**D-B. Bolt Definition of Done (DoD).** These hold for every bolt and are checked in its review. They are not story ACs, and the intent closes them:
- AC-16.1 (`build-copilot.mjs --check`), closed by ITEM-124;
- AC-16.3 (no stack terms), closed by ITEM-122. This is new: §16.1 names no closer for it (R-2);
- AC-16.5 (`node tools/validate.mjs`), closed by ITEM-124;
- AC-12.3 for any bolt that adapts specsmd material: the bolt appends its `ATTRIBUTION.md` line;
- AC-9.5's ATTRIBUTION rule for any change under `hooks/auto-handoff` or `hooks/keepalive`;
- the AC-15.8 marker on any legacy branch;
- the single-writer rule (§14);
- "done" means engineering done plus knowledge done (§14).

**D-C. AC-17.14 goes whole to engine story 001 (MAJOR; refine R-3).** A bolt has one unit and a story has one bolt (§4.1). R8 ("lens parts of AC-17.14 go to the lens") would therefore need a lens story inside an engine bolt. Proposal: engine owns AC-17.14 whole, and lens bolt 010 consumes findings (c), (e), (h) and (i) through `requires_bolts: [001, …]`. R8 is a product-owner decision, so product-owner must confirm this reading.

**D-D. Sequencing defect in the sweep (CRITICAL; refine R-1).** Bolt 011's check (§15) searches `docs/` and `README.md`. Measured on the worktree:
- 1,431 files under `docs/handoffs` contain old names or legacy paths;
- so do 14 under `docs/architecture`, 26 under `docs/product`, 14 under `docs/specs`, plus `docs/backlog`, `docs/plans`, `docs/plugins.md` and `docs/follow-ups.md`;
- `README.md` has 100 hits for `ai-dlc@ai-dlc-agent-workflow` or `/ai-dlc:`.

AC-17.4(d) allows history only under `docs/handoffs/`, "because the other history paths are outside the search scope". That is true only after ITEM-121 has moved them. So in W5 bolt 011 cannot pass, and CI runs the test command, so the branch would go red. The fix needs no AC change:
- 011 runs after ITEM-121;
- the reader pages pick up the new names in each bolt's own `docs` stage (R-9).

ITEM-122 already depends on both ITEM-117 and ITEM-121, which fits this order.
