Spec 001 §16 (revision r1) is superseded for bolt 015 and for the changed Requires cells of 009, 010, 011 and 014 until delta r3 is approved (see the informative note in the r1 delta header); G3 could have renumbered or rescheduled bolts that had not started; it approved the plan as written (D79).
