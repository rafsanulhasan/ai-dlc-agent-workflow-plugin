The lens workflow view draws **one flowchart per top-level step** of a run, counted from the run folder's record files (a run with steps 1–29 has 29 charts). Inside a step's chart every sub-step hangs under its parent step (28.1–28.6 under 28; loop rounds under the step they re-run). Settings stay.

**Why:** the human, 2026-10-24, after two attempts at "one chart per run" (steps 19.6.1.l3, 27.1): "nope it does not look good at all. keep the settings. but do what i say … there should be 29 different flowcharts." The earlier one-chart-per-run reading was mine, built on an answer to a question I framed.

**How to apply:** for lens layout requests, restate the exact picture (what is one chart, what connects to what) in the human's own terms and confirm it before routing; don't merge charts beyond what the human said. Related: [[related-work-parent-run]].
