## Human answer to 28.1's open questions (2026-10-23): "Re-read each turn"
At every `turn.start`, keep-alive re-reads its settings (`.ai-dlc/cache-keepalive.json` and the `env` of both settings files, with the same precedence and trust rules as at a Stop) and the machine override, then refreshes what it publishes. So a fresh session publishes its real settings from its first turn, and a machine override another session changed is picked up at this session's next turn. No polling while idle.
- Keep the cost small and safe: reuse the existing settings loader; a read that fails keeps the last good values and goes to the debug log, never throws; running tracks still change at their next ping.
