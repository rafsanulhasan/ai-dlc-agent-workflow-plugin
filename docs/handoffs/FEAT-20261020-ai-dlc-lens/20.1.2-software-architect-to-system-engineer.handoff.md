## Fixed decisions
- The hook is a new `ui.render` hook on `AssistantMessage`, in `hooks/diagram.ts`. When a block has no closed fence, it calls `next(e)` unchanged.
- Placement is one layout setting. The default puts the picture after its fence. The fallback puts it beneath the reply. OQ1 at G2 chooses.
- The renderer is a `node` child process, `renderer/render.mjs`, with vendored bundles: beautiful-mermaid with ELK, and MathJax `tex2svg`. Its output uses the `{ data, error }` envelope on stdout.
- PNG rendering is CONDITIONAL on ITEM-053.a. The preference is the resvg WebAssembly build; the fallback is the native per-platform resvg-js.
- PlantUML runs only with a trusted jar (`plantumlJar`, plus `javaPath` defaulting to PATH), under the security profile. The profile is CONDITIONAL on 053.a, and the fallback is to strip includes.
- Offline: sanitize input and output, and expose no host settings.
- Output by surface: the terminal gets an `Image` PNG of at most 2 MiB. Other surfaces get an `Svg` of at most 131,072 characters. Every failure falls back to the source plus a one-line reason. If the hook throws, it calls `next(e)`.
- Settings: `diagramMermaid`, `diagramMath`, `diagramPlantuml`, `plantumlJar` and `javaPath`, each with an `AI_DLC_LENS_*` variable.
