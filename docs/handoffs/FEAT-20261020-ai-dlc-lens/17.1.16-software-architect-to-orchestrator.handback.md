   - The new spec adds two sections:
     - L7, a window. The lens has no time window today; its "window" settings are context windows. Proposed: presets This session / Today / 7d / 30d / All, default 7d, the choice kept for the session only.
     - L8 and L9, the root-22 leftovers ITEM-073 and ITEM-074.
   - **Part A review: accepted with three amendments, no AC changes:**
     - D5: the session switch overrides the per-track `<track>.enabled` only, never the master `enabled`. As drafted, D5 contradicted D10 and Q-SL4.
     - D9: the surface signal exists, through `$.session.surfaces()` and `e.surface`. The lens already uses it at `register.tsx:1006`.
     - D10: the switch is kept in `$.store` per session id, like the `--session` overrides (`keepalive.ts:443`), not in module memory. Otherwise a `/reload-plugins` would forget an `[off]` and the track would re-arm and spend. AC-17.6.17 (no settings-file write) still holds.
2. **Q-CL9 (data access):**
   - **Prices:** already solved. The lens's existing `loadCosting` (the `costingPath` setting, default `.ai-dlc/model-metadata.json`, relative to the cwd) and `lookupModel` (`lens.ts:279`) match ids case-insensitively, drop `[1m]` and a trailing `-YYYYMMDD`, and check `id` and `aliases`. No match means unpriced. The only change is pricing each write by its TTL part.
   - **Transcripts:**
     - The folder is the parent of `transcript_path`, taken from `classic.SessionStart` (else Stop or UserPromptSubmit). This handles `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` without guessing at folder names.
     - `$.fs.read` cannot read the transcripts: it rejects files over 4 MiB and has no range read. 16 of the 609 transcript files are over 4 MiB (largest 10.5 MB, 754 MB in all).
     - So a plain-JS `node` child reader (`hooks/cache-reader.mjs`) streams the files, dedupes by `message.id` (182 duplicate ids in the largest file), emits numbers only, and pages its output under the 4 MiB stdout cap.
     - A truncated output is an error, never a partial figure.
     - Files older than the window start are skipped, and results are cached in memory by (path, size, mtime).
3. **ADRs:**

It records why Hit %, Tokens Saved and Median baseline deliberately differ from claude-lens.
   - **Session switch: no ADR.** It is scoped to one session, cheap to reverse, and follows the `--session` precedent.
   - **Formula reuse: no separate ADR.** It is not hard to reverse, and it is folded into ADR-0006.
