## Round 6 (44.2.l1.6): R44.17, R44.18 (ai-dlc-lens `hooks/register.tsx`)
- **R44.17, the race (from the code, intermittent live):** round 5's `followSeat` deferred its acts with `$.clock.after(0)`. That only moves a write past the render that asked for it. The host refuses a state write while *any* `ui.render` is being dispatched (d.ts 3494). Renders here are frequent: each step of a resize, the 500 ms blink (`:3939`), the 2 s poll (`:3943`). So the timer could fire during a later render's dispatch, and `seatChanged`'s first write, the place (`:1521`, then `update(viewA …)`), was refused: "seat follow: … state.set: denied". The same race applies to the band's hand-over write and to `pingBandSoon`'s write.
- **Fix:** `afterRender` (`:1483`; `AFTER_RENDER_TRIES` 20, `AFTER_RENDER_RETRY_MS` 100, `:1480`). It runs the act just after the render. When the host refuses the write as made during a render (`isRenderDenial`: "ui.render is being dispatched" or "drawing is pure"), it tries the whole act again 100 ms later, up to 20 times. Any other error, and the last refusal, still go to `lensFail`. Every act given to it is safe to repeat.
  - It is used by the seat follow (`:1517`), the band's hand-over (`:1505`, kept to one at a time by `bandGivingWay`, `:1496`) and the band ping (`:1558`).
- **R44.18, caused by R44.17 (traced in the code; the human saw it clear with the error):** narrow, `/ai-dlc:lens` leaves the pane in its minimised list (`commandOpenView(…, 'inline', …)`, lens.ts:2106-2109). On widening, `seatChanged` writes the place first. When that write was refused, the whole act stopped before the workflow layout (`commandOpenView(…, 'dock', w)`), so the docked pane kept the minimised list, with no step chart. The next seat change that got through drew it.
