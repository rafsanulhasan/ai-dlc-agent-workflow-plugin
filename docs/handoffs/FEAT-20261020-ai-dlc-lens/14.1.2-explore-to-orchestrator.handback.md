**2. Auto-handoff session naming** (`e:/Projects/personal/ai-dlc-agent-workflow-plugin/plugins/claude/ai-dlc/hooks/auto-handoff/`)
- config.ts:412 builds the name: `sessionName = (prefix, base, n) => \`${prefix}: ${base} (Session ${n})\``.
- config.ts:417-423: `baseName()` removes the `(Session \d+)` wrapper and throws the number away.
- register.tsx:429 sets `const depth = own ? own.depth : 1`, where `own` is `lineageOf()` (:357-359). That reads the in-memory `lineage` or the store key `lineage:<id>` (:345-354).
- register.tsx:436 takes the name from `own?.baseName ?? baseName(title, prefix) ?? short(id)`.
- register.tsx:449 renames the old session with `sessionName(prefix, name, depth)`.
- register.tsx:996-998 gives the new session `prior + 1` from the old session's stored lineage, and :1005 names it.
- Why "Session 2" produces "Session 2" again: N comes only from the lineage record, never from the title. A session titled "… (Session 2)" with no `lineage:<sessionId>` entry is treated as a new chain at depth 1. Typical cases are a session started or resumed by hand, a different session id, or a store entry that is missing. That session is renamed "(Session 1)" and the new one becomes "(Session 2)".
- Test auto-handoff.test.tsx:1766-1774 confirms this is the coded behaviour: a session titled "(Session 7)" is renamed "(Session 1)" and the next is "(Session 2)". Lines :1752-1763 show a proper chain going 1→2→3→4.
- Related tests: config.test.ts:110 and :115-117.
- When lineage exists but has no `depth` (written by an older version), no rename happens at all (:449, :1004).
