**R44.17: the race.**
- Round 5's seat follow deferred its writes with `$.clock.after(0)`. That only moves a write past the render that asked for it.
- The host refuses a state write while any render is being dispatched (d.ts 3494). Renders happen often here: every step of a resize, the lens's 500 ms blink (`register.tsx:3939`) and its 2 s poll (`:3943`).
- So the timer sometimes fired during a later render, and `seatChanged`'s first write, the place update (`:1521`), was refused. That explains why the error came and went on its own. The band's hand-over write and `pingBandSoon`'s write had the same race.

**The fix (small).** `afterRender` (`register.tsx:1483`, limits at `:1480`) runs each such act just after the render.
- When the host refuses the write as made during a render, it tries the whole act again 100 ms later, up to 20 times.
- Any other error, and the last refusal, still show as a lens error.
- It wraps the seat follow (`:1517`), the band hand-over (`:1505`, at most one at a time through `bandGivingWay`, `:1496`) and the band ping (`:1558`). Each of these acts is safe to repeat.
