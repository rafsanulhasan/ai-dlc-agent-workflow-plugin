## Intent
At every turn start, keep-alive re-reads its settings (both settings files' `env` and `.ai-dlc/cache-keepalive.json`) and the machine override, then publishes. A fresh session publishes its real settings from its first turn, and another session's machine override change is picked up at this session's next turn. A failed read keeps the last good values and is logged, and nothing is polled while idle.
