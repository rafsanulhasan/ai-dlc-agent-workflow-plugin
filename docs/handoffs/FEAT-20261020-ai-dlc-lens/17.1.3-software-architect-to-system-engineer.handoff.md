- **The ledger.** The ledger is per (source, TTL). A read hit refreshes only a 5m entry, and only a 1h write moves a 1h entry. The ledger is cleared at `session.end` and `stopForHandoff`, never at a turn start.
- **Forwarding.** Every usage goes to keep-alive together with its `agentId`. Auto-handoff's own accounting keeps its `!e.agentId` filter. The fallback is the `SubagentStop` transcript.
- **The running line.** It keeps its own `warm until`, as in E6 and E7.
- **The first ping.** The line reads `ping 0/m, first ping at HH:MM`, where the time is `nextPingAt`.
- **Published state.** The change is additive and optional: `caches?`. The `pings` comment says "landed".
- **034.b is CONDITIONAL on 034.a.** The `pingSubagents` setting defaults to off. Pings are counted per round, and spend per ping.
